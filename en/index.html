Questions we care about

Attractive numbers can be public, but their limitations should be public too.

01

Causality ≠ Independence

Chronological correctness prevents look-ahead; it does not erase research selection.

02

Research ≠ Production

Matching totals can hide different event-level decisions, so production needs direct replay comparison.

03

Price Data ≠ Resolution

A strategy feed and the market's official resolution definition are not the same object.

04

Improvement ≠ Ship

A rule that looks good recently can still fail broader acceptance tests.
