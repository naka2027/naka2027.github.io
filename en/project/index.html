System

From candidate signal to real order

Automation is not “signal means order.” It is a chain of decisions that can reject each other.

01Market

Discover the active BTC 5m window, tokens and market state.

02Data

Validate freshness, continuity and state readiness.

03Signal

Multiple information families form a candidate rather than a direct order.

04Context

Market state and post-signal logic can keep, adjust or STOP the candidate.

05Risk

Dynamic sizing plus account, balance, allowance and duplicate-trade checks.

06Execution

Place, track, confirm, record and persist before moving to the next market.
